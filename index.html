Is this a digital membership card?

No. This is the directory linked from your card’s QR code. It doesn’t verify membership or replace your ISOC discount card. Ask the committee about obtaining a card.

What about halal information?

Food offers should only be published once the committee has checked the specific venue. Where relevant, the offer details show the basis for that check. A listing is not a halal certificate. Please ask the venue about current ingredients, preparation and any dietary requirements.

Do I need an account to use this page?

No. You can browse without signing in. Saved offers stay in this browser on this device, not in an ISOC account. External providers such as Student Beans may require their own sign-in.

An offer has changed. Who do I tell?

Please tell an ISOC committee member which business you visited and what changed. Include the date so the listing can be checked. The committee can update this page without changing the QR code.
